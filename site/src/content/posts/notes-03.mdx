title: "Notes № 3 — Stale-proof by design"
description: Building a site that survives six months of neglect.
publishedAt: 2026-08-01
kind: notes
---

Most personal sites rot. The "currently reading" widget from 2024 is still there in 2026, quietly lying.

The rule on this site: nothing claims "now". No currently-building, no live feeds, no now page. Dated posts are fine — a date is an honest statement. "Currently" is a promise I won't keep.

The cost is that the site is less impressive on any given day. The benefit is that it's never abandoned-looking, which is the more expensive impression to lose.
