Every version of this site has kept the same two lists: books I've rated and tools I use. Projects sections came and went. The lists stayed.

The reason is simple. Work samples age and get NDA'd. A decade of recorded judgment doesn't. If you want to know how I evaluate things, the [books](/books) page is more honest than any case study I could write.

The system behind it is one generic item with a category flag. Books and tools today; films and music when I get to them. Nothing autoloads — an item shows up where I put it, or it doesn't show up at all. Like this:
